# Supra Human App — Client Help & Troubleshooting Guide

**Get the most out of your Supra Human experience.**

Your Supra Human app connects you with your coach, your training plan, and the information that helps you understand your progress.

Occasionally, technology needs a little attention. If something isn't working as expected, start here. Most common issues can be resolved in a few simple steps.

And remember: you don't have to figure everything out on your own. Your Supra Human team is here to help.

**A quick note:** There are two similarly named apps. The new app is **Supra Human Inc**. The older app is called **Supra Human App**.

## Getting Started & Account Access

### Should I delete the old Supra Human app?

Not yet. Keep the old app installed until we officially confirm that it’s no longer needed. You don’t need the old app to use your new Supra Human experience. If you’ve already deleted it, don’t worry.

Contact our team if you have questions about your historical information.

### Is there a desktop, web, or iPad version?

The current Supra Human app is designed for iPhone and Android devices.

If you’re trying to access your account from a computer or iPad, contact our team for guidance on currently available access options.

## Training & Workouts

### My workouts aren’t showing up. What should I do?

Your training schedule is connected to the program and assigned by your coach.

If your workouts aren’t appearing:

1. Open the **Training** tab.  
2. Check the dates on your training calendar.  
3. Tap the date of your next scheduled session.  
4. Close and reopen the app if the screen doesn’t appear to have updated.

If you still don’t see your workouts, send your coach a message through the **Chat** tab. They’ll help check your program and ensure everything’s set up correctly.

**Remember:** Some programs are scheduled to begin on a future date, so an empty day doesn’t necessarily mean something’s missing.

### Can I add my own workout, run, walk, or cardio session?

Your ability to add independent activities depends on the features currently available in your app.

For now:

* Your coach can add a training session to your schedule.  
* You may be able to add an exercise to an assigned workout using **Add Exercise** in the workout preview before starting.  
* If you accidentally add a set, swipe left on the set to remove it, where supported.

If you want to record an activity that isn’t available in your app, let your coach know. They can help you determine the best way to track it.

### How do I finish or save my workout?

When you’re ready to complete your session:

1. Check off at least one completed set.  
2. Scroll to the bottom of your workout.  
3. Tap **Finish Workout**.  
4. Complete the workout rating and any notes you’d like to share.

**Need to stop before finishing?**

You don’t have to lose the work you’ve already completed.

1. Tap the back arrow.  
2. Select **Pause and Come Back**.  
3. Return to your workout when you’re ready.

Your logged information will remain available so you can continue where you left off.

### Why can’t I enter my weight or repetitions?

Some exercises are configured as text-based activities rather than repetition-based exercises.

If you can check off a set but can’t enter your weight or reps, your coach may need to adjust the exercise settings.

Send your coach a message explaining which exercise you’re having trouble with. They’ll help get it corrected.

### Why isn’t the timer appearing for my planks or other timed exercises?

Timed exercises need to be configured correctly in your training program.

If your plank, wall sit, or another timed exercise isn’t displaying a timer:

1. Check whether the exercise is showing repetitions instead of time.  
2. If it is, send your coach a message.  
3. Your coach can review the exercise setup.

#### What about the rest timer sound?

Check that:

* Rest Timer Sound is enabled. (More → Settings → Rest Timer Sound)  
* Your phone’s media volume is turned up.  
* The Supra Human app is open.

The rest timer sound currently plays through your phone’s speaker, not through connected earbuds.

### Why can’t I find the exercise I want when swapping an exercise?

Start by updating your Supra Human app to the latest available version.

Then:

1. Open your assigned workout.  
2. Select the three-dot menu for the exercise you want to change.  
3. Select **Swap exercise**.  
4. Search for the exercise by name.

If you still can’t find it, send us the exercise name and a screenshot of what you’re seeing. We’ll help determine whether another exercise is appropriate or whether the exercise library needs attention.

### Why aren’t my supersets or circuits showing as one group?

Supersets and circuits display according to how your coach configured the workout.

Look for the grouping label near the top of the exercise section.

If you believe exercises should be grouped together but aren’t, let your coach know. They can review the workout setup.

## Nutrition & Food Tracking

### MyFitnessPal won’t connect. What am I doing wrong?

The two most common connection issues are entering the wrong username or using your MyFitnessPal password instead of your diary key.

Here’s how to connect:

**Step 1: Find your MyFitnessPal username**

1. Open MyFitnessPal.  
2. Navigate to the More tab.  
3. Locate your username.

Use your username, not your email address.

**Step 2: Create a diary key**

1. Open MyFitnessPal settings.  
2. Navigate to Privacy or Sharing & Privacy.  
3. Find Diary Sharing.  
4. Select Locked with a Key.  
5. Create and save a diary key.

Your diary key can be something simple that you’ll remember.

**Step 3: Connect MyFitnessPal to Supra**

1. Open Supra Human.  
2. Go to More → Connected Apps.  
3. Select MyFitnessPal.  
4. Enter your MyFitnessPal username.  
5. Enter your diary key in the Password field.

That’s it\!

**Important:** Your diary key is private. Never send it to anyone in a message.

**Still having trouble?**

If you receive an authentication error, double-check your username and diary key. You can also try creating a new diary key.

If your Nutrition tab isn’t visible, see the next section.

### Where do I log food? My Nutrition tab is missing.

Supra Human offers two nutrition-tracking options:

* MyFitnessPal  
* Supra’s built-in Nutrition tracker

These options can’t currently be used simultaneously.

If you’d like to switch between them, our team can help.

**To activate Supra Nutrition:**

1. Message your coach and request access.  
2. Once access is enabled, fully close the app.  
3. Reopen Supra Human.  
4. Look for the Nutrition tab.

If MyFitnessPal is already connected, disconnect it by navigating to:

Nutrition → Manage MyFitnessPal → Disconnect.

**What is Supra Nutrition?**

It’s built-in food tracking that lets you record meals directly in the Supra Human app.

Depending on the features available to your account, you can search for foods, scan barcodes, or use Talk to Add.

If you need a particularly extensive food database, MyFitnessPal may currently be the better option.

### Can I create my own recipes, meals, or custom foods?

You can create custom foods using the Quick Add feature.

Here’s how:

1. Open the Nutrition tab.  
2. Select Log.  
3. Tap Quick Add.  
4. Select My Foods.  
5. Choose Create Custom Food.  
6. Enter the food information and save.

**A few helpful shortcuts**

* Previously scanned foods can be found under Quick Add.  
* Saved foods and meals can be added again without starting from scratch.  
* To copy a meal to another day, tap the three-dot menu next to the meal and select Copy To.  
* To remove an item from a meal, swipe left on that item.

### The barcode scanner or food information is incorrect.

Accurate food information matters because your coach uses your nutrition records to understand your habits and help guide your plan.

If something doesn’t look right, let us know.

Please send:

* A photo of the nutrition label.  
* A photo of the barcode, if applicable.  
* The food name and serving size.  
* A screenshot of the information displayed in Supra.

We’ll review the entry and help get the issue addressed.

If you’re entering a custom amount and the numbers don’t update as expected, include a screenshot of that, too.

## Health Data & Connected Devices

### My steps, weight, or sleep aren’t showing up.

Your wearable sends information to your phone’s health app, which then shares that information with Supra Human.

The connection generally looks like this:

* **iPhone:** Your device → Device app → Apple Health → Supra Human  
* **Android:** Your device → Device app → Health Connect → Supra Human

There are three places to check:

1. Is your device sharing information with Apple Health or Health Connect?  
2. Is your device selected as the preferred data source?  
3. Does Supra Human have permission to access the information?

**Start here:**

Open Apple Health or Health Connect and check whether the missing information appears there.

* If the information isn’t there, check your device’s connection settings.  
* If the information is there but isn’t showing up in Supra, check your Supra permissions.

For detailed instructions, see the Wearable Sync Guide at the end of this document.

**A few things to know:**

* Supra currently receives steps, sleep, and weight through supported connections.  
* Heart rate, body fat, and wearable workout imports aren’t currently available through this connection.  
* A manually entered weight can override the synced weight for that day.  
* Syncing isn’t always immediate. Allow time for your device and apps to update.

### My Garmin won’t connect. I can’t find Garmin in Connected Apps.

You’re not missing anything. Garmin information is shared through your phone’s health app rather than a direct Garmin connection in Supra.

* On iPhone, Garmin Connect shares information with Apple Health.  
* On Android, Garmin Connect shares information with Health Connect.

The important step is making sure Garmin is selected as the preferred data source for steps and sleep.

Follow the instructions for your phone in the Wearable Sync Guide.

**One more thing:** Garmin may not send information to Apple Health until Garmin Connect has been opened and your watch has synced.

If your numbers look low, open Garmin Connect, allow your watch to sync, and check again.

### Does Supra Human work with my Apple Watch? Can I see my heart rate during workouts?

Your Apple Watch can share supported health information with Supra through Apple Health. Currently, this includes:

* Steps  
* Sleep, where supported

The app doesn’t currently start workouts directly on your Apple Watch, display live heart rate during a Supra workout, or send Supra workouts to your Apple Fitness activity rings.

In the meantime, you can continue recording activities through Apple’s Workout app.

### Where are my body fat, lean mass, measurements, or resting heart rate?

Supra currently displays selected information from supported health connections, including steps, sleep, and weight.

Body fat, lean mass, body measurements, and resting heart rate aren't currently displayed through these connections.

You can continue recording this information in your device's app while using Supra for the information currently supported.

If you believe historical information is missing, contact our team so we can investigate.

## Progress & Historical Information

### My information from the old app isn’t showing up. Did I lose it?

Your historical information is important, and we understand how frustrating it can be when something you expect to see isn't there.

The migration from the old app to the new Supra Human experience is handled separately from your ongoing activity.

If something is missing:

1. Check your profile and Progress sections.  
2. Look for historical information under earlier dates or years.  
3. If it's still missing, contact our team.

Please tell us what information you're looking for, such as weigh-ins, progress photos, or workout history.

We'll check your records and investigate anything that hasn't transferred correctly.

**A note about timing:** The old and new apps don't continuously synchronize with each other. Information entered into the old app after your migration may not automatically appear in the new one.

### Where can I find my progress photos, weight history, and charts?

Your progress information is available through your profile.

**To find your progress photos:**

1. Tap your profile picture in the upper-right corner.  
2. Select Progress Photos.  
3. Choose the month and year you'd like to view.

To add new photos, tap Add.

To compare two dates, select Compare.

**To find your progress charts:**

1. Tap your profile picture.  
2. Select Progress.  
3. Choose Weight, Steps, or Nutrition.

**Missed your progress photos during a check-in?**

No problem. You can add them later through Profile → Progress Photos → Add.

**Can't find older information?**

Historical information may be grouped by year. Check earlier years before assuming something is missing.

If your photos or weight history still aren't appearing, contact support.

## Notifications & Communication

### How do I turn off group chat notifications but keep messages from my coach?

You can adjust your notification settings without losing access to your community groups.

Here's how:

1. Open More.  
2. Select Settings.  
3. Open Push Notifications.  
4. Find Network Updates.  
5. Select All Members.  
6. Set Notifications to None.

Repeat this for any other group you'd like to mute.

Keep Coach Messages enabled so you continue receiving notifications from your coach.

**Will I still see group messages?**

Yes. Muting notifications doesn't remove your access to the group. You can still open it whenever you'd like.

**Don't see a notification badge on your iPhone?**

Open your iPhone's Settings → Notifications → Supra Human and make sure Badges are enabled.

### Where do I find my coach chat?

Your one-on-one conversation with your coach is located in the Chat tab.

1. Tap Chat at the bottom of your screen.  
2. Look for your individual coach conversation above Supra Support.  
3. Your community groups appear below.

If Chat doesn't open or your messages aren't loading, fully close the app and reopen it.

If the issue continues, contact support.

# **Wearable Sync Guide**

## **Connecting your wearable to Supra Human**

Your wearable can provide useful information about your daily activity, sleep, and weight.

If that information isn't appearing in Supra, this guide will walk you through the setup.

### **First, understand how the connection works**

Most wearables don't connect directly to Supra Human.

Instead, they share information with your phone's health app.

**If you use an iPhone:**

Your wearable → Device app → Apple Health → Supra Human

**If you use Android:**

Your wearable → Device app → Health Connect → Supra Human

For the connection to work, each step needs to be set up correctly.

### **Before you begin**

Have these three things ready:

* Your wearable device.  
* The app associated with your device.  
* Your Supra Human app.

---

## **Part A: iPhone & Apple Health**

### **Step 1\. Make sure your device shares information with Apple Health**

Choose the instructions that match your device.

**Garmin**

1. Open Garmin Connect.  
2. Select More → Settings → Connected Apps → Apple Health.  
3. Turn on the information you'd like to share, including Steps, Sleep, and Weight where available.  
4. Follow the prompts to allow access.

Keep Garmin Connect open while your watch syncs.

**WHOOP**

1. Open WHOOP.  
2. Go to More → App Settings → Integrations.  
3. Select Apple Health.  
4. Connect and allow the requested permissions.

Use Apple Health as the connection to Supra rather than trying to connect WHOOP directly.

**Oura**

1. Open Oura.  
2. Navigate to Settings → Data Sharing.  
3. Select Apple Health.  
4. Turn on the information you'd like to share.

**Withings scale**

1. Open the Withings app.  
2. Go to Profile → Settings.  
3. Find Export Health Data to Apple Health.  
4. Allow Weight.

**RENPHO scale**

1. Open the Apple Health app.  
2. Tap your profile picture.  
3. Select Apps.  
4. Find RENPHO Health.  
5. Enable Weight.

**Hume scale**

1. Open the Hume app.  
2. Navigate to Profile → Connected Apps.  
3. Select Apple Health.  
4. Allow sharing.

For this connection, weight is the information currently supported in Supra.

**Apple Watch**

Apple Watch shares supported information with Apple Health automatically.

You don't need to set up an additional connection for this step.

**Fitbit**

Fitbit uses a different connection method on iPhone.

1. Open Supra Human.  
2. Go to More → Connected Apps.  
3. Select Fitbit.  
4. Follow the connection instructions.

One important limitation: a direct Fitbit connection and an Apple Health connection cannot currently be used simultaneously in Supra.

If you also use a smart scale that shares information with Apple Health, contact our team for help choosing the best setup.

### **Step 2\. Make sure your wearable is the preferred data source**

This is an important step, particularly if you carry your iPhone and wear a watch.

Your phone and watch may both record steps. Apple Health needs to know which source to prioritize.

To check:

1. Open Apple Health.  
2. Tap Browse.  
3. Select Activity → Steps.  
4. Scroll down to Data Sources & Access.  
5. Tap Edit.  
6. Move your wearable's app (Garmin Connect, WHOOP, Oura, etc.) to the top.  
7. Tap Done.

Repeat the process for Sleep:

1. Return to Browse.  
2. Select Sleep.  
3. Find Data Sources & Access.  
4. Tap Edit.  
5. Move your preferred wearable source to the top.

This helps ensure the information from your wearable is prioritized rather than being mixed with other sources.

### **Step 3\. Connect Apple Health to Supra Human**

1. Open Supra Human.  
2. Go to More → Connected Apps.  
3. Select Apple Health.  
4. Review the permission requests.  
5. Enable Steps, Sleep, and Weight where available.  
6. Continue through the permission screens.  
7. Choose the available history-sharing option.  
8. Tap Allow.

Make sure you complete the final permission screen.

### **Step 4\. Check Supra Human's Apple Health permissions**

1. Open Apple Health.  
2. Tap your profile picture.  
3. Select Apps.  
4. Choose Supra Human.

You can also find these permissions through your iPhone's Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices.

Make sure Supra Human has permission to read:

* Steps  
* Sleep  
* Weight

**Important:** Make sure you're selecting the new Supra Human app, not the older Supra Human App.

The new app may appear as Supra Human Inc.

### **Step 5\. Allow background updates**

Your phone needs to allow the apps to update information in the background.

1. Open iPhone Settings.  
2. Select General → Background App Refresh.  
3. Make sure Background App Refresh is enabled.  
4. Allow it for your wearable's app and Supra Human.

### **Step 6\. Refresh your information**

1. Open your wearable's app.  
2. Allow your device to sync.  
3. Open Supra Human.  
4. Go to the Today screen.  
5. Pull down to refresh.

Give the connection some time to update. Information may take a few hours to appear.

## **Part B: Android & Health Connect**

### **Step 1\. Find Health Connect**

Health Connect is the central place where your Android device can share health information between supported apps.

* On Android 14 or newer, Health Connect is generally built into your phone.  
* On Android 13 or older, you may need to download Health Connect from the Google Play Store.

To find it, open your phone's Settings and search for Health Connect.

### **Step 2\. Make sure your device shares information with Health Connect**

Choose the instructions that match your device.

**Garmin**

1. Open Garmin Connect.  
2. Go to More → Settings → Connected Apps.  
3. Select Health Connect.  
4. Turn on sharing.  
5. Allow Steps, Sleep, and Weight where available.

**Samsung Galaxy Watch / Samsung Health**

1. Open Samsung Health.  
2. Tap the three dots.  
3. Select Settings → Health Connect.  
4. Allow Samsung Health to share the available information.

**Fitbit / Pixel Watch**

1. Open the Fitbit app.  
2. Tap your profile picture.  
3. Navigate to Settings → Health Connect.  
4. Turn on Sync with Health Connect.  
5. Select Allow All or the relevant permissions.

Recent information may take approximately 15 minutes to appear in Health Connect.

**WHOOP**

1. Open WHOOP.  
2. Go to More → App Settings → Integrations.  
3. Select Health Connect.  
4. Allow the available information, including Steps and Sleep.

**Oura**

1. Open Oura.  
2. Go to Settings → Data Sharing.  
3. Select Health Connect.  
4. Enable the information you'd like to share.

**Hume scale**

Hume currently shares Steps and Sleep with Health Connect on Android.

Weight and body composition information aren't available through this connection, so you may need to enter your weight manually in Supra.

**Withings / RENPHO scales**

Open your scale's app and enable Health Connect sharing.

Make sure Weight is included in the permissions.

### **Step 3\. Make sure your wearable is the preferred data source**

If your phone and wearable both track steps, Health Connect needs to know which source to prioritize.

1. Open your phone's Settings.  
2. Search for Health Connect.  
3. Select Data and Access.  
4. Open Steps.  
5. Find Data Sources and Priority.  
6. Move your wearable's app to the top.

Repeat this for Sleep.

This is particularly important if your step count in Supra is significantly lower than the number displayed on your watch.

### **Step 4\. Connect Health Connect to Supra Human**

1. Open Supra Human.  
2. Go to More → Connected Apps.  
3. Select Health Connect.  
4. Allow access to Steps, Sleep, and Weight where available.

### **Step 5\. Check Supra Human's permissions**

1. Open Health Connect.  
2. Select App Permissions.  
3. Find Supra Human or Supra Human Inc.  
4. Make sure Steps, Sleep, and Weight are enabled.

Make sure you're granting permissions to the new Supra Human app, not the older Supra Human App.

### **Step 6\. Allow background updates**

Android phones sometimes limit background activity to preserve battery life.

If your information isn't updating:

1. Open your phone's battery settings.  
2. Find the battery optimization options.  
3. Review the settings for your wearable's app, Health Connect, and Supra Human.  
4. Allow the necessary background activity where supported.

If you've recently updated Android, check your permissions again. Some updates can change app permissions.

### **Step 7\. Refresh your information**

1. Open your wearable's app.  
2. Allow it to sync.  
3. Open Supra Human.  
4. Go to the Today screen.  
5. Pull down to refresh.

Allow time for the information to transfer between apps.

## **Part C: Common wearable problems**

**My steps are lower in Supra than on my watch.**

Your phone may be prioritizing its own step count instead of your wearable.

Return to the Data Sources & Priority settings in Apple Health or Health Connect and move your wearable's app to the top.

**My steps look low in the evening but correct themselves later.**

Some wearable devices don't finish syncing immediately.

Open your device's app and allow it to sync. Check Supra again afterward.

**My information is in Apple Health or Health Connect but not Supra.**

Check that Supra Human has permission to access the missing information.

If permissions are already enabled, try disconnecting and reconnecting the health connection in Supra.

**My weight stopped updating.**

Check that:

* Weight sharing is enabled in your device's app.  
* Supra Human has permission to read Weight.  
* You haven't manually entered a weight for that day.

A manually entered weight can override the synced value.

**My sleep information isn't appearing.**

Check that your wearable is sharing Sleep with Apple Health or Health Connect.

Then verify that Supra has permission to read Sleep.

**I only see permissions for Supra Human App.**

That's the older app.

Make sure you've connected the new Supra Human app and granted its permissions separately.

**I can't find Garmin, WHOOP, Oura, or my scale in Connected Apps.**

That's expected for devices that share information through Apple Health or Health Connect.

Connect Apple Health or Health Connect instead of searching for a separate device connection.

**Connected Apps won't open, or See All Apps doesn't work.**

Close and reopen Supra Human.

If the problem continues, contact support and include a screenshot.
