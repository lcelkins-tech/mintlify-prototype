# **SUPRA HUMAN**

# **New Client Onboarding — Coach \& Support Playbook**

**Version 2.0 · Draft for Product \& Coaching Review**

**Content type:** Internal coaching and support reference  
**Primary audience:** Coaches and Client Success representatives  
**Secondary audience:** AI/support agent  
**Purpose:** Help the team guide new members through onboarding, identify the right next step, and direct questions to the appropriate person or resource.

\---

## **How to use this resource**

Use this playbook when a coach or CS representative needs to help a new member get started with Supra Human.

It is organized around common onboarding situations rather than a member-facing sequence. Find the situation that matches the question, review the recommended action, and use the member-facing instructions where helpful.

**Important distinctions**

* **Coach-owned:** Personalized goals, training, nutrition expectations, check-in cadence, and coaching feedback.
* **Support-owned:** App access, account issues, technical troubleshooting, and platform navigation.
* **Product-dependent:** Feature availability, integrations, app behavior, and release-specific functionality.

Do not assume every member has the same setup. Availability may vary by account, coaching plan, device, app version, and membership.

# **1. Member needs to download the app and sign in**

**Common questions**

* How does a new member access the app?
* Where is the download link?
* What email address should they use?
* What should they do if they cannot sign in?

**What to know**

After account activation, the member receives a welcome email from Team Supra Human. The email includes links to download the app.

* Android: Google Play
* iPhone: Apple App Store

The member should sign in using the same email address associated with their activated account.

**Recommended next steps**

1. Confirm the member's account has been activated.
2. Ask them to locate the welcome email from Team Supra Human.
3. Have them download the Supra Human app using the appropriate app-store link.
4. Confirm they are using the email address associated with their account.
5. If access still fails, direct them to reply to the welcome email or contact the appropriate support channel.

**Member-facing guidance**

"Check your welcome email from Team Supra Human for the app download link. Once you've downloaded the app, sign in using the email address associated with your activated account. If you run into trouble, let us know so we can help."

**Escalate when:** The welcome email is missing, the account is not activated, or the member cannot access the account after following the instructions.

# **2. Member needs help understanding the onboarding call**

**Common questions**

* What should we cover during the first coaching call?
* What information should be established before the member begins?
* What should the member expect after the call?

**Purpose of the onboarding call**

The initial coaching call establishes the starting point for the member's coaching experience and begins the process of creating a personalized plan.

**Topics to cover**

* Personal details and goals
* Starting point and relevant data to track
* Initial training and nutrition plan
* Check-in and communication cadence
* How the plan may be adjusted as the member progresses

The coach uses this information to establish a baseline and create a plan tailored to the member. As more information becomes available, the coach and member can refine the plan.

**Recommended next steps**

1. Establish the member's goals and starting point.
2. Explain which information and habits will be tracked.
3. Review the initial training and nutrition expectations.
4. Explain how and when the member should check in.
5. Clarify how the member should communicate with the coach between scheduled calls.
6. Make sure the member understands their first priorities.

**Important:** This resource does not prescribe a universal onboarding-call script or a standard coaching protocol. The coach should follow the member's actual program and established Coaching team expectations.

# **3. Member is unsure what to do first**

**Common questions**

* What should a new member complete after onboarding?
* What should they focus on during their first week?
* How do we prevent a new member from feeling overwhelmed?

**What to know**

The coach provides the member's initial priorities after the onboarding call. Tasks should reflect the member's goals and personalized plan.

**Examples of initial tasks**

### **Record weight**

A member may be asked to record their weight each morning on an empty stomach.

Consistent measurements help establish a baseline and make it easier to review trends over time.

**Coach action:** Explain the frequency and method expected for that member. Confirm the member knows where to record weight in the app.

### **Track nutrition**

A member may be asked to log food in the Nutrition section of the app.

Consistent tracking helps the coach understand current habits and make informed adjustments.

**Coach action:** Explain what the member should track and how consistently. Confirm which nutrition-tracking option is enabled for the member.

**Product note:** The Supra Human Nutrition Tracker may need to be enabled by a coach before it appears. If the member cannot access the expected nutrition features, verify their setup before escalating.

### **Complete the initial check-in**

The member may be asked to complete an initial check-in and upload starting progress photos.

These provide a baseline for reviewing progress over time.

**Coach action:** Explain which information and photos are requested and how the member should submit them.

### **Review the personalized plan**

The coach may send the member an in-app message containing:

* A written overview of the plan
* A personalized video walkthrough
* Initial action items
* Instructions for using the app

**Coach action:** Confirm the member received the information and understands what to focus on first.

# **4. Member needs to understand the coaching rhythm**

**Common questions**

* How often should a member hear from their coach?
* Where should a member submit updates?
* How should a member communicate between calls?

**What to know**

A member's coaching experience may include:

* **Weekly coaching calls:** Connection with the coach over Zoom.
* **Weekly check-ins:** Member submits updates through the app and receives coach feedback.
* **In-app messaging:** Communication with the coach between calls.
* **Personalized feedback:** Guidance and next steps based on progress.

The actual cadence and expectations depend on the member's individual program.

**Recommended next steps**

1. Explain the member's scheduled coaching-call cadence.
2. Show them where to complete their check-in.
3. Explain how to message their coach.
4. Clarify when and how they should expect feedback.
5. Reinforce the best communication channel for questions or updates.

**Do not assume:** Every member has the same check-in day, call frequency, or response expectations. Confirm the individual coaching arrangement.

# **6. Member cannot access Nutrition or expected nutrition features**

**Common questions**

* Why can't the member see the Nutrition tab?
* Why are expected food-logging features missing?
* How should the member begin tracking food?

**What to know**

The Nutrition tab is used to record meals and review daily calorie and macronutrient intake.

Available methods observed during the app walkthrough included:

* Quick Add
* Type a Meal
* Talk It Out
* Scan Barcode
* Nutrition Facts

Members may also have options to copy meal information, add meals, and remember a preferred logging method.

The Supra Human Nutrition Tracker may need to be enabled by the coach before it becomes available.

**Recommended next steps**

1. Confirm whether the member should have access to Nutrition.
2. Verify whether the Supra Human Nutrition Tracker needs to be enabled.
3. Confirm which nutrition-tracking method the member is expected to use.
4. Walk the member through the available logging options.
5. If the feature should be available but is not appearing, escalate the access or technical issue.

**Important:** Do not assume all members have the same tracker or logging methods. Confirm the member's configuration.

# **7. Member needs help finding or completing a workout**

**Common questions**

* Where does the member find their training schedule?
* How do they start a workout?
* What if a workout is missing?
* What if they are unsure how to perform an exercise?

**What to know**

The Training tab provides access to the member's workout schedule and training information. The Today tab may also display the scheduled workout and a Start Workout option.

**Member-facing steps**

1. Open the Today tab.
2. Find the scheduled workout.
3. Select Start Workout when the session is available.
4. Follow the exercises in the session.
5. Record sets, reps, and weights as instructed.
6. Use Exercise Swap if the option is available and appropriate.
7. Complete the workout in the app.

**Coach action**

If the member cannot find a workout, is unsure what to do, or needs a change to the program, review their assigned plan and provide guidance.

**Important:** The coach determines the member's training plan. Do not recommend changing or substituting a prescribed workout without appropriate coaching guidance.

# **8. Member needs help connecting a device**

**Common questions**

* Where can a member connect a wearable?
* Which integrations are available?
* What should they do if data is missing?

**What to know**

Supra Human can use supported connected apps to bring certain activity and health data into the member's experience.

The initial app walkthrough showed connection options including:

* Fitbit
* Health Connect
* Oura
* Zepp

The available integrations and data depend on supported connections and the member's setup.

**Member-facing steps**

1. Open the Today tab.
2. Find the connected-devices section.
3. Select Manage Connected Apps.
4. Review the available connections.
5. Follow the prompts to connect or manage the device.

**If data is missing**

1. Confirm the member has connected the correct app or device.
2. Check whether the required permissions have been granted.
3. Confirm which data the integration is expected to provide.
4. If the issue continues, escalate to Supra Support or the appropriate Product contact.

**Product validation required:** Confirm the current integration list, supported data categories, connection instructions, and differences between iOS and Android before this content is treated as approved guidance.

# **9. Member cannot find or complete a check-in**

**Common questions**

* When does the check-in appear?
* Where does the member complete it?
* What if the check-in is missing?
* Where can the member find coach feedback?

**What to know**

Check-ins give the coach a consistent view of the member's progress and help inform adjustments to the plan.

During the initial app walkthrough, a check-in notification appeared at the top of the Today tab on Monday. The schedule may differ depending on the member's coaching plan.

**Member-facing steps**

1. Open the app.
2. Look for the check-in notification.
3. Open the check-in.
4. Complete the requested questions.
5. Submit the responses and any requested photos or other information.
6. Review coach feedback when it becomes available.

Coach feedback may include written communication and video.

**Coach action**

* Confirm the member's check-in schedule.
* Follow up if a scheduled check-in is missing.
* Review submitted information.
* Provide feedback through the appropriate coaching channel.

**Escalate when:** The member cannot access or submit an expected check-in, or a technical issue prevents completion.

# **AI / SUPPORT AGENT RETRIEVAL GUIDANCE**

**Primary retrieval intents**

* New member onboarding
* Download app
* Sign in
* Welcome email
* First coaching call
* First-week priorities
* Weight tracking
* Nutrition setup
* Nutrition tab missing
* Coach communication
* Weekly check-in
* Workout schedule
* Missing workout
* Connect wearable
* Missing device data
* Progress photos
* App settings
* Educational resources
* Support escalation

**Agent response behavior**

1. Identify whether the question is about coaching, app navigation, account access, or a technical issue.
2. Retrieve the specific section relevant to the question. Do not return the entire playbook when a focused answer is sufficient.
3. Give the coach or CS representative the next action first.
4. Include member-facing wording when it would help the representative communicate the answer.
5. Do not invent account-specific details, feature availability, coaching expectations, or technical behavior.
6. If the answer depends on a member's setup, ask the representative to verify that setup.
7. Escalate questions that require a coach's judgment, account access, technical investigation, or Product confirmation.
8. Do not present draft or unverified information as approved policy.

**Human ownership boundaries**

* The AI agent can surface approved information and help locate instructions.
* The coach remains responsible for personalized training, nutrition expectations, and coaching decisions.
* Support remains responsible for technical and account issues within its remit.
* Product owns confirmation of feature behavior, integrations, and release-specific functionality.
